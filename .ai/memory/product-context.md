# Product Context

- PolyBob is a personal quantitative trading system for Polymarket and related crypto/onchain signals; research and paper execution are validation layers on the path to real trading.
- It is scoped for a single operator, not as a public SaaS product.
- The product goal is to discover, validate, risk-control, and execute strategies with real cost-adjusted profit potential; research, paper trading, and review exist to improve real trading decisions.
